# Doddle OS Frontend

The web frontend of DoddleOS, the agentic marketing operating system from DoddleHQ.

Operators sign in, open a workspace, and hand marketing work to specialized AI agents. Runs appear on a board where humans review, steer and approve everything before it ships.

## What is inside

- Sign in and sign up with email or Google
- Workspaces that keep each brand or client separate
- Agent modules including Content Factory, SEO Cluster, Business Audit, Social Growth, Ads Execution, Analytics Action, Email Nurture and Brand Guardrails
- The DoddleOS v3 desktop experience with a home dashboard, launchpad, agents roster, assignments board, triage queue, blueprints library and reports
- Brand persona and data connection settings

Documentation for the v3 pages lives on the oCode wiki under Agents / Doddle-OS / v3-frontend.
