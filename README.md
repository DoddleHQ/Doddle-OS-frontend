# Doddle OS Frontend

The web frontend of DoddleOS, the agentic marketing operating system from DoddleHQ.

Operators sign in, open a workspace, and hand marketing work to specialized AI agents. Each run is visible while it happens, so humans can watch the work, steer it, and approve what ships. Nothing goes out without a person saying yes.

## What is inside

- **Sign in and sign up.** Create an account with email and password or continue with Google. Sessions stay signed in and can be renewed without asking again.
- **Workspaces.** Every brand or client gets its own workspace, so work, connections and settings never mix between customers.
- **Agent modules.** The fleet covers the whole marketing floor. SEO Cluster researches keywords and builds briefs. Business Audit checks a site end to end. Content Factory plans and writes landing pages and site content. Social Growth runs the calendar and the posts. Ads Execution handles Meta and search campaigns. Analytics Action turns Google and merchant data into next steps. Email Nurture moves leads through sequences. Brand Guardrails reviews output against brand policy. SaaS Launch Ops tracks launch milestones and rollout.
- **The DoddleOS v3 desktop.** A calm desktop-style home with a launchpad for starting new work, an agents roster with live chat, an assignments board tracking every run, a triage queue for everything waiting on a human decision, a blueprints library of reusable playbooks, and reports of finished work.
- **Review and triage.** Finished runs land in the triage queue with their full history. Approve them, send them back with change requests, or report them.
- **Settings.** Set the brand persona the agents write within, and connect data sources such as Google Search Console, Google Analytics and Merchant Center.

## How work flows

An operator describes an outcome on the dashboard or the launchpad. The system matches it to a blueprint and suggests the agents for the job. The run appears on the assignments board and in the agent chat while it works. When it finishes, it waits in triage until someone approves it. Approved work lands in reports next to everything else that shipped.
